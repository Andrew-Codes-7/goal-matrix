Start with one goal.

Shohei Ohtani wrote his goal in the middle of a 9×9 grid, then filled the eight boxes around it with what would get him there — and broke each of those down again. Do the same here.

How it works

Click the gold centre to edit your goal. Click any of the eight boxes to open it.

Inside a box you get another eight — the specific things that make that one happen.

At the bottom level you write actions. Tick Track daily on any action and it shows up under Today.

The whole thing
Every box on one page, the way Ohtani drew it. Click any square to jump to it.

AI assist

Paste an Anthropic API key to let Claude suggest supporting goals and actions. It's stored only in this browser, on this computer, and is sent straight to Anthropic — nowhere else. The app works fine without it; you just type everything yourself.

Don't have one? An API key is separate from a Claude.ai subscription — paying for Claude doesn't give you one. Get it at console.anthropic.com → API keys → Create key, and add a few dollars of credit under Billing. It starts with sk-ant-. Filling a whole matrix costs a few cents.
